Scrap salvage parts

What "Fill scrap" tries to recover from the offcut, biggest first. Crates are cut from the same 19mm melamine, so these come off the sheet you are already cutting — and every job can place any crate size: this is using up leftover, not a parts list for the job on the bench. Qty is how many you want in total across this nest, not per sheet; set one to 0 to leave it out.

🔗 rows come from the real crate product, so they are cut with their grooves. A row with no link is only cut to size — the panel comes off blank.

Sheet materials
